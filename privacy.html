Clap! Clap! Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 3, 2026

Clap! Clap! is a simple rhythm game. This page explains what the app does with your information, which is easy to summarise: it doesn't collect any.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. It does not ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

The app remembers your best score, unlocked partners, stickers, and settings so they're there the next time you play. This information is stored only on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else, and deleting the app removes it.

Internet access

The app works fully offline and makes no network requests.

Children

Clap! Clap! is suitable for all ages. Because the app collects no information at all, there is nothing collected from children either.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new date.
